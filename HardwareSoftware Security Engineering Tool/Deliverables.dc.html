Rendering rules
How the source becomes the document the customer accepts
Follow the DID format for each artifact
Section order, paragraph numbering, and front matter come from the data item description cited on the CDRL
Refuse to render stale content silently
A block flagged stale either blocks the render or renders with a visible change bar — never quietly
Carry the trace into the document
Evidence identifiers and WBS references print as they appear here, so the assessor can follow them back
Record the submittal
Every render is hashed and logged with its revision, so a resubmittal can be diffed against what was sent
