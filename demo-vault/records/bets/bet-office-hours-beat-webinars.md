# Office hours beat webinars

Scheduled webinars ask people to show up at our time. Recurring office hours ask them to show up at theirs.

**We win if** attendance per session is lower but total monthly attendance is higher, and qualified leads per attendee holds.

**We lose if** office hours become a support queue with no pipeline value — which is the failure mode worth watching for, because it will still *feel* busy.
