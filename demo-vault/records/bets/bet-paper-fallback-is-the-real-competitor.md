# Our competitor is paper, not the other vendor

Crews that churn do not move to a competing product. They go back to a clipboard.

**Settled won.** Exit interviews on the pilot: five of six lapsed crews reverted to paper, one moved to a spreadsheet. None evaluated another vendor.

The consequence is that "faster than the alternative" has to mean faster than paper, which is a much harder bar than faster than software.
