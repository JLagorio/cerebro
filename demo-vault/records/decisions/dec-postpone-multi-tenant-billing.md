# Postpone multi-tenant billing to next quarter

## Context

Two enterprise prospects have asked for consolidated billing across subsidiaries. Neither has signed.

## Decision

Do not build it this quarter. Revisit when a signed contract depends on it.

## Consequences

Risks losing one of the two deals. Building it now would take roughly the whole of [[epic-first-week-activation]], which serves every customer rather than two prospects.
