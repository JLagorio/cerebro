# Keep the crews we win

Acquisition is not our problem this quarter — the field app lands well and the launch pipeline is full. What we cannot yet show is that a crew still uses it in week six.

This objective is deliberately about *retained* usage, not signups. A crew that installs the app and reverts to paper has cost us more than one that never signed up.
