### Reading this

The nesting is one grouping chain, not a special layout: objectives, then the
key results that point at them, then the work those key results claim.

Both directions of link are followed, which is the point — a tree should not
care which side of a relationship the data happens to live on.

### When it is wrong

An objective with no key results under it is not on track, it is unmeasured.
The tree cannot tell you which, and neither can anyone who has not asked.
