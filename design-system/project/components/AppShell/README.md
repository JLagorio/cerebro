# AppShell

The window: a persistent icon rail, the context sidebar beside it, and a broad canvas that meets the window's right and bottom edges.

- Use it once per window; every place renders inside its canvas. Left to right: `AppRail`, `Sidebar`, then the main column holding the canvas row (page panes, and at its right the record panel and the assistant) over the status bar.
- Geometry: the shell is a full-height row on `surface-navigation`. The rail is `rail-w` (54px) on `surface-frame`; the sidebar `sidebar-w` (240px, resizable 180 to 460px) on `surface-navigation`; the canvas takes the rest on `surface-app` (`n-0`). Width budgets reserve no outside frame: no gutter, no border.
- The sidebar and canvas start 10px below the window's top (`--shell-top-inset`, 6px under 760px). The canvas has one rounded corner, 16px at its top left (12px under 760px), resting on the sidebar's background; its right and bottom edges meet the window square.
- The canvas holds up to four page panes, each at least 400px with its own history and editor; a modified click or Shift+Enter opens beside, and narrow windows park panes rather than closing them.
- A page in a pane is a page frame: `PageToolbar` (64px) over the page. A document's reading column is `surface-readable` (880px) with `surface-inset` (32px) sides and 40px above, its title in the `page-title` role (40/48, semibold, -0.025em) and its body in `prose` (16/28).
- The assistant defaults to floating and rests minimized as one 48px round button 20px from the canvas's right and bottom: `surface-raised` on `border-subtle`, `shadow-lg`, a 22px `sparkles` in `text-ai`. Working, an arc turns on its edge; waiting on the person, a `warn-500` dot lands on its shoulder. The page keeps 100px of scroll clearance under it, and a docked side panel at the canvas's edge moves it beside the panel.
- Expanded, the assistant floats over the page (up to 640px wide, 24px radius) without taking canvas width. Floating, the docked panel and the roomy canvas presentation are one instance, so its thread, draft, turn and pending approvals survive every change.
- When the sidebar folds, every destination, New, search, the assistant, theme and Settings stay on the rail, which adds Show sidebar. Under 1048px the sidebar opens as a drawer from behind the rail; narrower bands step it down to the rail so a record and the assistant both fit.
- This card is a composition of `src/App.tsx`, `src/app/AppRail.tsx`, `src/app/Sidebar.tsx`, `src/pages/DocPage.tsx` and `src/agent/AiPanel.tsx`'s markup and classes (`src/app/shell.css`, `pageFrame.css`, `pageToolbar.css`, `surface-system.css`), with the reading column and resting assistant rules copied from `src/pages/pages.css`, `src/editor/editor.css` and `src/agent/chat.css`, because the shell reads app state.
