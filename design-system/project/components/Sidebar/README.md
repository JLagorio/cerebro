# Sidebar

The context sidebar beside the rail: the vault's name, one heading for the area the rail picked, that area's rows, and a footer for sync.

- Use it for the area's own shelf: Home's destination rows, Collections and Favorites; Pages' All pages, Recents, Collections or Folders (one at a time, by `pageTreeMode`) and Favorites; Knowledge's folders; the Agents roster; Databases with their counts; Work's repositories. Inbox, Settings, Work and an open Studio project lend their own navigation in its place, and the canvas assistant takes priority over all of them.
- Geometry: `sidebar-w` (240px) by default, resizable from 180 to 460px by the `ResizeHandle` on its right edge; `surface-navigation` with no border, 10px below the window's top (6px under 760px). Header 52px: a 26px `r-md` vault tile on `n-100`, the vault's folder name at 15px semibold `n-800`, and Hide sidebar (28px, `panel-left`).
- The contextual heading is 14px medium on a 24px line, 6px 20px 8px padding. Knowledge, Agents, Databases and Work give it their fold and actions: its chevron shows while shut and, while open, only under the pointer or focus; actions are 24px with 13px glyphs, revealed the same way (`cb-reveal` on `motion-pop`). Pages' heading is a plain label; Home has none.
- Rows are `cb-nav-row`: `nav-row-h` (36px), 0 10px padding, `r-md`, `sp-2` gap, `fs-ui`/`lh-ui`, a 15px glyph. Resting `n-700` regular with an `n-100` wash on `motion-hover`; selected `n-200` fill, medium `n-900`, `aria-current="page"`. Focus is a 2px `border-focus` outline inset 2px. An agent's row wears a synapse `bot`; a paused one dims to `n-500` with a 12px `pause` tail.
- Counts are quiet facts: mono `text-2xs` tabular numerals in `n-500`, `cortex-600` when the count wants acting on (Inbox), never a filled pill, and spoken in the row's name ("Inbox (6)").
- Sections fold under a 12px medium `text-meta` header whose one chevron turns 90° on `motion-turn` (`cb-chevron`); what they hold drops in under it (`cb-fold-in`). An empty section says so in words ("No favorites yet").
- The Folders tree draws `cb-tree-row`s indented 14px a level: a 13px `n-400` chevron and 14px `folder`/`folder-open` in `n-500` for folders, files inset to line up past the chevron. The open page wears the row selection; New page and Options appear on hover or focus.
- The footer (min 52px, 12px 14px padding) holds the quiet sync badge only when it has something to act on: "3 uncommitted", "1 to push", "Up to date" with a remote. With nothing to say it collapses.
- Hide sidebar folds it; the rail keeps every destination, New, search, the assistant, theme and Settings, and adds Show sidebar. Under 1048px it opens as a drawer from behind the rail, over a scrim.
- This card is a composition of `src/app/Sidebar.tsx`, `src/app/SectionHeader.tsx` and `src/components/FileTree.tsx`'s markup and classes (`src/app/shell.css`, `src/styles/surface-system.css`), with live `Icon`, `IconButton` and `Tooltip`, because the component reads app state.
