### M60.1 Computed values resolve before filter, sort, group, chart and dashboard

- **What:** DATA-3. `viewFilters.fieldValue`, `surface.sortValue` and `grouping.groupEntries` read raw frontmatter, so created and edited time, rollups and derived relations read empty, though the filter menu offers them. `computeChart` and `dashboardNumber` read `e.properties`, so a rollup can't be summed, and chart.ts draws a band with no number as 0 (`value = n ?? 0`). Filter reads a frontmatter `title:` first; sort reads the page title first.
- **Files:** `src/engine/{types,schema,properties,viewFilters,surface,grouping,chart,dashboard,createRecord}.ts`, the seven matching engine tests, `src/views/{BoardView,ChartView}{,.test}.tsx`, `e2e/computed-values.spec.ts` (new).
- **Approach:**
  - **One read.** `Schema.fieldValue(e, field)`, in one pinned order: `VIRTUAL_FIELDS` (`title`, `type`, `createdAt`, `modifiedAt`) as entry facts; then a computed kind (created_time, last_edited_time, rollup, a relation or person with `from` through one `derivedStems` that `resolveField` shares); then `e.relationships`; then `e.properties`. Absent is `undefined`, never 0. A per-schema `WeakMap<Entry, Map>` memo keeps sorting cheap.
  - **Rollups are numbers.** `RollupCalcMeta.yieldsNumber` (count, sum, avg, min, max) gives a finite Number or undefined; earliest, latest and show keep text. `sum` over nothing numeric returns '' like the other three (CV-05).
  - **Every reader.** evaluateFilters (its local `fieldValue` goes), `sortValue`, `groupEntries`, every measured read in chart.ts and `dashboardNumber` call `schema.fieldValue`. A band with rows but no number is skipped before its hue is stamped and counted in `ChartData.unmeasured`; a groupBy part with no number draws no segment; the caption says "1 band without a number".
  - **Computed bands are read-only.** `bandDef` and `bandWritable(def)`: false for a computed kind or a `from` side; an undeclared field is writable. `createTarget` seeds nothing there, and BoardView refuses the drop with one toast and patches nothing.
  - **Release note** in the commit body: saved views on computed fields return different rows, and `title` filters read the page title. "Within 7 days" is `on_or_after` a date until M61.3's relative operators. Fixtures sit at T12:00:00Z with bounds a day away, so they hold after .3 reads instants as local time.

### M60.2b checkView: dangling fields, options and operators are named, never an empty view

- **What:** DATA-1, the check half.
