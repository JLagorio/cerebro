## Decisions

- Empty chat groups greeting, composer and suggested prompts near the center.
  A conversation with messages keeps its composer below the scrolling thread.
  These are CSS presentations of the same controller and input.
- The 54px rail and 240px default context width remain. Their surfaces feel
  continuous; context captions, decorative chevrons and repeated branding
  give way to useful navigation. Selected states and focus stay distinct.
- Home keeps the domain-defined lane order. Its assistant entry is centered,
  quick actions are quiet, and Recently learned follows the work lanes.
- Full-page prose gets a more comfortable 16px reading scale and spacing.
  Compact/section editors, code, tables and custom block carriers stay scoped
  separately. Existing title scale and page-width preferences remain.
- Library uses a centered shelf capped at 1120px, with aligned title, filters,
  search and cards. Cards emphasize purpose and retain permission/duty facts.
  The recipes offer still discloses vault-read behavior and Gmail draft behavior.
- Page-header actions have consistent 32px minimum height and rounded shape;
  dense controls inside views are unchanged.
