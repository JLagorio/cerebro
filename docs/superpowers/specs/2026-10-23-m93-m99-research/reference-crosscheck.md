The [core](final-core-audit.md), [actions](final-actions-audit.md) and
[product](final-product-audit.md) audits preserve exact inspected lines and more
adopted/rejected examples. The coordinator additionally read AppFlowy's
[save-to-page service](../../../examples/AppFlowy-main/frontend/appflowy_flutter/lib/plugins/ai_chat/application/chat_edit_document_service.dart)
and Macro's [first-output projection](../../../examples/macro-main/crates/agent_session/src/domain/service/first_output.rs).
The [final manifest](final-review-manifest.json) fingerprints the complete union
of reported inspected files. Hash capture is a final checkpoint, not proof every
reviewer saw an atomic repository snapshot.

No reference establishes that Cerebro must replace React, adopt a hosted service,
change its guarded knowledge model or erase YAML indiscriminately. The concrete
lesson across them is to connect persistence, state ownership and recovery to the
same visible user journey; polished controls alone do not provide that contract.
