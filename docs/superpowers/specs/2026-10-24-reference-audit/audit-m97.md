## Risks the references expose

- **In real vaults, rich reveal is often unsupported.** Any mark, link, `_`, `&`, table, toggle, CRLF or multi-line paragraph anywhere refuses the whole page. A third of demo pages fail the grammar alone. Browser journeys pass on a two-paragraph fixture, and mention linking inherits the same limit. Outline and Tolaria avoid this by deriving the map from their own serializer.
- **Nothing produces `cited` for chat answers** (assembly answers cite manifest items). M97.4 will either ship the chat role empty or derive it from reply wikilinks or the first-`via` list. That is AFFiNE's footnote failure: a search hit counted as a citation.
- **Model text can become the owner's word.** Save answer writes no provenance, and ingest registers unmarked pages as `human:owner`. `ingest/source.rs` closes that self-ancestry path for `knowledge/`; Save answer reopens it, and `cache_source` copies already use it, because ingest never reads their `generated` stamp.
- **Injection coverage is narrower than it reads.** The offline harness proves effects stay blocked, not that the model ignores injected text. Cerebro frames untrusted text per tool; AFFiNE states the rule in its one chat prompt, and Activepieces tests it against the live model.
- **A timeout is reported as unsupported.** If the editor initializes slowly, reveal says the content does not support exact passages, which is false.
- **Silent caps recur in two references:** AFFiNE's backlinks (100 per linking page on desktop, 5 on web), dropped selections and rerank fallback, and Morrow's 20-mention limit. n8n is the counterexample: its knowledge search returns `limit`, `hasMore` and `truncated` (`N8/packages/cli/src/modules/agents/agent-knowledge-retrieval.ts:232-266`). Every new KNOW surface keeps counted truncation, and unavailable never reads as empty.
- **Live evaluation spends the person's own quota and can grade Claude with Claude.** Activepieces caps cost and separates model-drafted labels; M97.7 does neither yet.
- **A pipeline-derived map leans on BlockNote internals.** Pin it with upstream-version regression tests (the TOL-02 pattern), so an upgrade fails loudly instead of silently falling back to `unsupported`.
