## Verdict

- **What Codex adds:** it treats the clipboard as a carrier route (cut only after the write lands, a typed private payload, quoted TSV, and no paste that changes the schema). It also adds resource faces and a contract for each component. Following its rows into today's tree turns up 8 gaps that neither audit named:
  - the record-table cell clipboard;
  - type-to-edit;
  - multi-select outside tables;
  - a face for a missing vault image;
  - the cost of board snapping and erasing;
  - narrow board toolbars;
  - the outline disagreeing with section links;
  - attachments in exports.
- **Where ours is stale or wrong (4 places):**
  - AFN2-06 aimed M97.2 at `revealBlock`, which serves section links, instead of `sourceReveal`.
  - We called AFFiNE's synced doc editable in five places (affine-2 Avoid, BL-154, the backlog reject row, and `dimension-features.md` E5 and its reject row). It is a read-only store.
  - BL-134's composition gate was met on 2026-10-25.
  - BL-166 cites a raw write that is now a create-only `applyBatch`.
- **Where ours is stronger:**
  - Codex misses every P0/P1 editor defect that is still open in code: serializing on every keystroke, database-fence loss, the ⌘U and paste routes, Enter under IME in open menus, filter seeding, M97 reveal, the board locator, fold memory and the ContextMenu layer.
  - Its "retain" verdict on AE03, AE07 and AE17 is contradicted by `claims.ts:23`.
  - Seven of its rows (six "adapt", one "reject") describe behaviour Cerebro already ships.
