## Verdict

- **What Codex adds:** failure semantics traced to source: paste and export loss (AF06–10), debounce versus reload (AF18), transfer and migration (AF40–46). It also finds two small editor gaps we missed: case-sensitive find and a nested outline. Its breakdown yields three more: ⌘K search that silently skips unreadable notes (agent `search_notes` already counts them), a Quick Open miss that never mentions Recently deleted, and comma-separated input in multi-select.
- **Where ours was stale or wrong:** `composition="utility"` now has a consumer. Prior AF-02, AF-03 and AF-10 were overtaken by the M95 archive (M95.3 decision), the M97.1/M97.2 locators plus BL-95, and M99's selected store. Two citations have drifted.
- **Where ours is stronger:** role tokens with measured contrast and an adoption ratchet, the settings kit, restore in place, subject-keyed events, the render-fault P0, per-command AI landing sets and page lock. Codex has no row on any of these.
