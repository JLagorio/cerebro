## Verdict

- **Codex adds:** lifecycle and completion facts we never traced: duplicate provenance, import attachment acquisition, export's missing-blob substitution and attachment authority. Checked against the current tree, these give 5 real Cerebro rows (below), one of them a live defect: Duplicate copies import identity stamps, so a copy can take over a re-import.
- **Where ours is stale or wrong:**
  - BL-16 says "Rust makes no HTTP call". That is stale: two hardened `reqwest` transports now exist.
  - OUT1-18 overstates Outline's plugin isolation: per plugin on the server only; the client survives a failed plugin only because nothing awaits the load.
  - OUT5-16 reads the 1 KiB webhook body cap as bounded evidence. The cap is applied after an unbounded read.
  - OUT3-06 presents Outline's "Clear cache" as safe. It wipes all browser storage.
  - Every other Cerebro claim was re-checked and still holds: BL-01, -03, -24/-111, -39, -52, -88, -130 are still open, and the counts are still exact.
- **Where ours is stronger:** we found concrete mechanisms Codex does not have:
  - abilities in the scan DTO;
  - the selected-store `vault_writes` relay gap;
  - a pipeline-derived source map with byte-preserving rich save;
  - ContextMenu layering;
  - the agent-reach move delta and the changed-since line;
  - the 2026-07-28 MCP adapter and the find-and-cite Skill.

  Codex's register covers every Outline family, but most of its dispositions read "retain" or "defer".
