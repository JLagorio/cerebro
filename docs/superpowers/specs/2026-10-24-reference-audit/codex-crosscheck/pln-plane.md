## Verdict

- **What Codex adds:** the work-management half of Plane that we skipped: planning states, sequences, completion, cycles, intake, saved-view authority, background jobs (P01–P14, P34–P37, P62–P66). It points at three live Cerebro defects without sizing them: a saved view drops a rule it cannot parse and the next save erases it; item keys can be reused after Trash; version restore's Cancel does not cancel. It also adds failure-as-zero counterexamples.
- **Where ours is stale:** the 10-22 dossier's PL01/PL02/PL03/PL04/PL06 premises are overtaken by M94.4, M96 and M99, but `specs/2026-10-23-m93-m99-research/traceability.md:171-176,285` already recorded that, and our audit read it (audit-m98.md:5). Tree movement after 10-24 made BL-169 ("no connections UI") and BL-16 ("Rust makes no HTTP call") stale. BL-186 ("ViewProblems shows stale fields") rests on a partial premise. None of our Plane reference claims turned out wrong.
- **Where ours is stronger:** Plane's design-system layer and render containment, which Codex's chapter never cites. Also BL-15's reconcile mechanism. Several Codex "adapt" rows describe things Cerebro already ships: physical-key fallback, guarded paged pickers, disabled-row skipping.
