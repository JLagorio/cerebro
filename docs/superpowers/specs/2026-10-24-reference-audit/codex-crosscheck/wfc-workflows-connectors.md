## Verdict

- **Codex adds:** a status vocabulary in which each word describes its evidence; compatibility of parked runs across a format bump (WC-11); a dependency readout before an account is removed (WC-14); and connection availability split into separate facts (WC-12). The breakdown scan adds two gaps that neither audit raises: workflow waits never reach "Waiting on you", and GitHub `429` responses fence a repository.
- **Ours is stale:** both status lines predate the build. 26 `workflow_*` commands, the Library UI, the V47 origins and both fixed HTTPS transports now exist. BL-18 and BL-22 are overtaken, BL-16 mostly (two duplicated builders, no clippy ban), and BL-15 is half done. BL-19, BL-102, BL-103 and BL-184 diverge from the implementation defaults. A04's claim that definitions hold no secrets is unproven.
- **Ours is stronger:** it gives a concrete exit for a lost create, typed vault refs, pinned targets and taint, the breaker and lane coupling, single-instance, approvals held in the renderer, and the CV-11 comment. Codex is stale too: it reports no workflow UI or IPC, but `src/lib/ipc.ts:2171-2199` and `src/library/Workflow*.tsx` now exist.
