## Capture something you can check

Open the assistant and name the page or source you want it to learn from. Ask it to explain the claim and keep the evidence with the concept. An ordinary note and a knowledge concept have different purposes: a concept is a claim you can review as its sources change.

## Review the evidence

1. Open Knowledge, then Review. Open a concept from the queue.
2. Read the claim. Open Details to inspect its evidence and follow the relevant source.
3. Check that the source supports the claim, including its date and any qualifications.
4. Use Verify in the review bar only when you have checked it yourself. Verification records your confirmation; an agent's confidence is not your review.

If the claim is wrong or its evidence is unavailable, leave it unverified and ask the assistant to correct or recheck it. A proposed change may wait for your approval. Review what will change before allowing it.

Knowledge has Concepts, Review and Activity tabs. Activity explains recorded changes and background work. An unavailable section means its read failed; it does not mean there is nothing to review.
