## Start with a paused agent

Open the agent's page and check Instructions, Reads, Writes in and its tool permissions. Set its Schedule in properties, then choose Done. Inspect the displayed schedule before resuming the agent.

In Settings → Knowledge, Learn on its own controls background work and is off by default. With it off, schedules do not fire. Enable it deliberately when you are ready for background work in this vault.

## Check what happened

Keep Cerebro open and your computer awake for scheduled desktop work. This is not an always-on cloud scheduler. An overdue schedule is not a promise to replay every missed occurrence.

Open the agent's Runs tab and Knowledge → Activity to inspect recorded attempts and outcomes. Waiting, skipped, failed and completed work are different states. If an approval is waiting, review its target before allowing it.

Pause the agent or turn off Learn on its own when you want to stop new scheduled background work. Inspect an already running task separately; changing a schedule is not an undo of earlier actions.
