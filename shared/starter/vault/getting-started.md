# Getting started

Your pages live as markdown files in this folder. Write a page for a thought,
plan or reference. Databases gather pages that share fields: open [[tasks]] to
try the table and board, then change their fields or make a database of your own.

## Capture first

Use New for a quick thought. Drop files into the Inbox, then file them when you
know where they belong. Your original files stay yours.

## Give an agent a job

[[task-helper]] adds context to a task you assign through its Agent field.
[[weekly-notes]] writes a short note each Friday. Both start paused: open their
pages, read their instructions and grants, then press Resume when you want them
to work. You can edit their instructions, pause them again or remove them.

## Ask the Assistant

Open the Assistant from the sidebar to ask about your pages, find something,
or work through an idea. You can keep writing while it runs and return to the
same conversation later.

Type /catch-me-up or /what-i-did to the Assistant for a read-only recap. Their
instructions are editable skill files in the Library.
